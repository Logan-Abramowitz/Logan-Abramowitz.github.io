## About Me


![Profile Picture]({{ "assets/images/profile-pic.jpg" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

My name is {{ site.name }}, and I am a senior at Cornell University double majoring in Computer Science and Operations Research & Information Engineering (ORIE). I’m especially interested in using data, optimization, machine learning, and computational tools to solve real-world problems and improve how systems operate.

At Cornell, I’ve had the opportunity to apply these interests across a variety of projects. I worked with the ORIE department on a project to improve the efficiency of Cornell’s North Campus Mailroom and later on a research project focused on predicting demand for a local food pantry following its relocation. I’ve also explored machine learning through a Formula 1 project, where my team developed models to predict pit stop decisions and provide insights into race strategy.

Most recently, I spent the summer of 2026 as an Analytics & Insights Intern with DIRECTV’s Ad Sales organization. During my internship, I developed an analytics framework using geospatial and Census data, worked across several teams on AI and workflow automation projects, and presented recommendations to senior leadership. I also led a team that won DIRECTV’s intern hackathon by developing a prototype focused on improving live sports discovery.

Previously, I interned at StratusX, where I worked on cloud infrastructure and cybersecurity, and at Stand Out for College, where I developed tools for collecting and processing college admissions data. At Cornell, I also served as Vice President of the Cybersecurity Club, helping grow the organization from roughly 10 active members to more than 60 and serving as Interim President for several months. These experiences have strengthened both my technical skills and my ability to work with and lead teams.

Outside of academics and work, I love spending time with my friends, whether that’s bowling, having a catch, playing wiffle ball, or staying inside for a board game. I also enjoy building just about anything, from computers and furniture to my all-time favorite: Legos.
